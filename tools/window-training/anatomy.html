Anatomy & Terms

Every instruction on this site uses these words. Ten minutes here saves an hour of confusion on the wall.

The window, part by part

Step through the groups below. The frame is what stays in the wall; the sashes and beads are what come out; the trim is what you take off and put back.

The pocket — the part that decides everything

Cut horizontally through the window and look down at one side jamb. This is the view that matters, because the space between the inside stop and the blind stop is the pocket — the slot your new insert has to live in. If it is too shallow, an insert will not work here at all.

Check the pocket before you quote the job

A shallow pocket is the one thing that stops an insert dead. Measure it on the first visit, before anyone promises the customer a price. If it will not make depth, the honest answer is a full-frame replacement.

What the new unit brings with it

An insert is a complete window in its own frame. It has no nailing fin — it is built to be screwed through its jambs into your old frame and covered by the stops.

Terms, in one place

TermWhat it isWhy you care
Side jambThe vertical board on each side of the frame.Width is measured jamb to jamb. This is the surface your screws land in.
Head jambThe horizontal board at the top.Height is measured up to this. The head expander closes the gap here.
SillThe sloped board at the bottom, outside the stool.It slopes, so heights get measured from its high point.
Inside stopRemovable strip holding the lower sash in from the room side.First thing off, last thing back on. Save it — new stock rarely matches.
Parting beadThin strip in a groove separating the two sashes.Must come out before you measure. Leaving it in makes the opening read narrow.
Blind stopFixed strip at the exterior edge of the jamb.The outer limit of the pocket, and what the new unit pushes up against.
PocketThe clear space between blind stop and inside stop.If it is under about 3¼″, an insert will not fit.
StoolThe interior shelf — what most people call the sill.Stays put. Your unit lands behind it, not on it.
ApronTrim under the stool.Only comes off if you are replacing the stool.
Insert / pocket windowA complete window built to drop into an existing frame.What this whole site is about. No nailing fin, no siding work.
Full frameTearing the old frame out back to the rough opening.The fallback when the pocket is too shallow or the frame is rotten.
