Vinyl Replacement Windows

Measuring, prepping, and installing an insert window inside the original frame — the full process, start to finish, with every step drawn out.

Read this before you open a window

Assume lead paint on anything built before 1978. The moment you disturb a painted surface on a pre-1978 home you are under the EPA's Renovation, Repair and Painting rule: certified renovator on site, plastic containment, no dry scraping or sanding, HEPA cleanup, and the signed paperwork in the file. Old windows are the single most common source of lead dust on a job.

Also every time, no exceptions: eye protection when you pry or cut, cut-resistant gloves any time you handle a sash with glass in it, and never let a sash sit unsupported in the opening. Old glazing lets go without warning.
